# GeoWild animal sources

Reviewed: 2026-10-23. This release contains 100 species. `data/animals.json` is canonical and generates `site/js/animals.js`. Range evidence and documented source corrections are recorded in `data/range-evidence.json`; independent country-coordinate fixtures are in `data/country-anchors.json`. The original 17 source notes remain below, followed by the additional 83 records.

## Range policy

Answers accept every listed current native country, including conservation reintroductions within a native range. They exclude zoo locations, introduced populations, vagrants and historically extirpated countries. Country polygons are a learning-game approximation, not exact habitat boundaries. No marine or seasonal-migration cases are included.

## Photographic use

All photographs are locally packaged JPEGs, resized from the identified Wikimedia Commons files to a maximum dimension of 960 pixels for the original 17; new photographs are resized to 800 pixels. No generated animal images are used. The files retain their listed Creative Commons licenses; resizing/recompression is the only GeoWild alteration. The vicuna source was already cropped on Commons. Captive photographs identify the species and do not supply native-country evidence. Attribution in the game must retain the author, license and linked Commons source.

## Packaged asset validation

All 17 local photographs were opened for visual inspection on 2026-10-22. The visible animals match their Commons species identification. No browser/gameplay QA is claimed by this data check.

`python3 scripts/download-animal-assets.py` validates JPEG start/end signatures, decodes dimensions through macOS `sips`, requires a maximum side of 600–1000 px and a minimum side of 400 px, and rejects files over 400,000 bytes. Existing assets are never overwritten; missing assets are downloaded using verified Commons metadata with the recorded license. The initial run downloaded and validated all 17 successfully.

A Node VM load of the actual `site/js/animals.js` passed: 17 unique IDs, the frozen object fields, local JPEG paths, complete source/photo fields, English display text and no CJK characters, distinct ISO3 country codes, and valid HTTPS source URLs. The bank covers 23 distinct countries. Total packaged image size: 3,702,220 bytes. Missing native-range sources: 0. Missing photographic licenses: 0. Unusable or missing packaged images: 0.

## Expanded-bank verification

Mammal country lists were compared against the primary [MDD v2.5 dataset](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17033774), with uncertain entries excluded and six source-specific corrections. Five non-mammals use their government, museum or specialist conservation sources. Exactly 100 distinct species are packaged. The answer unit is a source-supported country, including native-range reintroductions, not a precise habitat outline. French Guiana maps to the bundled FRA country polygon and is identified explicitly in answer text.

Coordinates use Natural Earth label points as independent test inputs. Its offshore New Zealand and Equatorial Guinea labels failed the point-in-country test and were replaced with independently sourced Rotorua (Te Papa Museum) and Evinayong (GeoNames) settlement coordinates. No production polygon, scoring threshold or accepted-country list was weakened for those failures.
