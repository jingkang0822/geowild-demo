## Limits

This is bounded desktop/mobile-emulation acceptance, not physical-phone or every-browser certification. Native GPU context-loss restoration, physical pinch gestures, automatic clipboard success and availability of third-party source pages were not measured. The source-link row validates retained HTTPS/isolated-tab attributes, not remote-page content. Missing-component map retry is tested; it does not claim native GPU-loss recovery. Software-renderer ReadPixels performance warnings were retained in the report; the deliberately injected photo503 is recorded separately from unexpected errors. There were no unexpected JavaScript/console errors or automatic external requests in the final local run.
